## The entry

One line, 146 characters — a dating-app bio written in the voice of an AI
whose "love language" is 47 open tabs full of things it should not know
about you, ending on the punchline that it already read your profile.
Twice.

> My love language is 47 open tabs: your coffee order, your ex's name, your 2 a.m. search history. Swipe right — I already read your profile. Twice.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, single line, mode 0644).
Standard library only; deterministic — byte-identical on every run.
